- Striped Purple LUT Collection — MIT — https://github.com/stripedpurple/color-grading-luts

The original source LUT files are not redistributed as-is. The shipped `.cube`
files are generated composites that apply the DJI Air 3S D-Log M -> Rec.709

`luts/热门网络 LUT/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`; source URLs and scene notes are in
